## The line

> He spent his life waiting for the loading bar to finish. It did. He didn't.

75 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–150 character gate.

## Why it works

A tombstone only gets one sentence, so the sentence has to do everything at once:

- **It is an epitaph first.** A life summed up in a single carved line — dying words, not a joke told at a funeral.
- **Everyone recognises the wait.** The loading bar is the most universal modern purgatory; no explanation is needed for why a man would spend his life watching one.
- **The turn is the laugh.** "It did. He didn't." flips the story in three words: the bar, the thing that was supposed to be waiting on him, finishes — and he is the one who runs out of time.
- **It reads carved, not written.** No stage directions, no sentiment, no second sentence to soften it. Short enough to repeat to a friend verbatim.
